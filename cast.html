Case File #002

THE CAST

Eight names. One island. Nobody's telling him the same story twice.

He counts eight people living in that house, including himself. That's already one more than he can keep straight — and trust, once you start handing it out here, doesn't come back easy.

01 — Protagonist

The Man

No name he can remember

Pulled out of the surf with nothing on him but a headache and the clothes he woke up in. He arms himself early. He doesn't sleep well. And every kindness the family offers him buys them one more night of his cooperation.

The amnesia's genuine. What it's covering for isn't as innocent as he'd like. Full context in the backstory →

Trust
Threat
02 — Eldest Daughter

Romi

Bold, and she knows it

First face he sees that reads as anything close to normal. Sharp mouth, flirts like it's a reflex, doesn't flinch when he pushes back. She and her little sister Yumi go at each other constantly — whether that's a real rivalry or just noise, he can't tell yet.

She breaks the house's unwritten rules more than anyone. Either she's the one person being honest with him, or she's got the least riding on staying in character.

Threat
03 — The Boy

Jinu

The one nobody accounts for

A quiet kid who has no business being on this island — glimpsed in dreams more often than in the flesh, tied to a fenced-off pen out back that the family steers every conversation away from.

Say his name at dinner and watch how fast the subject changes. That reaction alone tells you more than anyone's willing to say out loud. Full context in the backstory →

04 — Patriarch

Father

Calm, devout, in charge

Doesn't raise his voice because he's never once needed to. Runs the household on scripture and runs the island's hunts with the same flat conviction — and everyone under his roof works hard not to disappoint him.

He's also the one who eventually goes looking for what the family's been sitting on. What he finds is the closest thing this story has to an answer. Full context in the backstory →

05 — Matriarch

Mother

Particular about everything

Generous at the dinner table, exacting the second the plates are cleared. Her warmth runs on a rhythm, like she's hosting for an audience only she can see.

Whatever looks like a contradiction in this house, she's usually the first to smooth it over — often before he's even finished noticing it.

Trust
Threat
06 — Elder Son

Minu

Big, and easily rattled

Built like he could end a fight in one move, and rarely acts like it. Corner him and he lashes out fast; give him room and he backs off just as fast.

He's the household's tell. Watch what makes Minu nervous — it's usually worth being nervous about yourself.

Trust
Threat
07 — Youngest Daughter

Yumi

Small, sharp-eyed, sharper-tongued

Youngest in the house, least interested in keeping anyone comfortable. Quick with an insult, quicker to clock what the adults are choosing not to say.

She and Romi bicker nonstop, but it's Yumi who tends to blurt out the thing the rest of the family is tiptoeing around.

08 — The Employee

Tae-hee

Quiet, watchful, wary

Works the guesthouse and says almost nothing about himself while he does it. He clocks more than anyone gives him credit for, and carries a grief the family never puts a name to.

Catch him alone and he's mid-conversation with someone who isn't in the room. Full context in the backstory →
