Episode 01 — Washed Ashore

She waves through the fence like she's already expecting you.

He has no memory of his own name. What he does have is a shoreline, a battered mailbox with nobody's mail in it, and a guesthouse run by a family that took him in without asking a single hard question. That's the first thing that should have worried him.

The welcome is warm. The rooms are clean. And somewhere in the back of his mind, a version of events keeps surfacing that doesn't match the smiles at the dinner table.
