Case File #000 — Overview

A guesthouse with no other guests.

PIGPEN starts small: a man on a beach, salt water in his lungs, no memory past the last few minutes. It ends somewhere much worse. In between is a family that's a little too good at making a stranger feel at home, a boy in a pig mask nobody wants to talk about, and a mystery that keeps folding back on itself the harder he pulls at it.

Written by Carnby Kim (the author behind Sweet Home) with art by Beom Sick Cheon. Complete at 68 episodes — this site works through the story, the cast, and, if you want it, the ending. Pick a file below.
