The companion release

Same house. Different controller.

8BiT Softworks built this alongside the manhua's team, translating the guesthouse into a top-down pixel world where every conversation is a small bet on who's telling the truth.

Trust Is a Resource

Every choice nudges a hidden meter for each family member. Push too hard and doors start staying locked. Push too little and you never learn anything worth knowing.

The House Changes at Night

Rooms rearrange once the lights go down. What was a hallway at 6pm might be a dead end by midnight — the game doesn't warn you, it just lets you find out.

Multiple Ambiguous Endings

No ending confirms everything. Like the source material, 8BiT's version leaves you with a version of events you have to decide whether to believe.

A Soundtrack That Lies to You

Calm music plays over rooms that are anything but. The score was built to undersell danger on purpose — read the silence, not the melody.

Genre: Pixel Horror Perspective: Top-Down Playtime: 4–6 Hours Developer: 8BiT Softworks Based on: PIGPEN by Carnby Kim & Beom Sick Cheon
